minishogi is a mini-variant of the japanese board game shogi. but to make it more simpler, i have removed the drop rule logic.

 basically you capture the enemy king by moving your pieces. each piece has its own unique property.

 the one with the crown is the king. That's the most valuable piece. The white/black squares next to them indicate what team they are in. (this is a 2 player game) 

when the piece goes to the opposite rank, they will promote. Promotion will upgrade the piece’s movements.
